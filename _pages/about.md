Turning **Data into Decisions** that Matter.

I'm a Senior AI/ML Engineer at [Dune Security](https://www.dune.security/), where I build LLM agents for security red-teaming, from multi-channel conversational agents to real-time voice agents. Previously, I built RAG and recommendation systems at [New York University](https://www.nyu.edu/) and LLM tools for clinical use at [Johnson & Johnson](https://www.jnj.com/).

I hold an MS in Electrical Engineering from [NYU Tandon](https://engineering.nyu.edu/). There I researched mmWave channel sounding at [NYU WIRELESS](https://wireless.engineering.nyu.edu/) with Prof. [Sundeep Rangan](https://engineering.nyu.edu/faculty/sundeep-rangan), and co-authored the Springer textbook [_Fundamentals of Communication Theory_](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-20615-2) with Prof. [Unnikrishna Pillai](https://engineering.nyu.edu/faculty/unnikrishna-pillai). Before that, I researched space-to-ground quantum key distribution at [ISRO](https://www.isro.gov.in/).

**Interests:** LLM agents, retrieval-augmented generation, voice AI, recommendation systems, and machine learning for wireless communications.
